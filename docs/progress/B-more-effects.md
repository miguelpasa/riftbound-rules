# B - More card effects (R3 widening)

Goal: raise the number of fully supported cards by parsing the next most frequent effect sentences. Method: `npm run cards`, then a frequency pass over the sentences of the remaining `partial` spells and over unit/gear trigger sentences (reminder text in parentheses and keyword tokens removed, split on '.', numbers replaced by N). The parser stays all-or-nothing: a card only becomes `full` when every sentence parses.

Engine plumbing:

- `effects.ts`: `targetRequirements(effects)` derives the slots a spell needs; `spellTargetsLegal` validates a choice (used by `play.ts`); `enumerateSpellTargets` lists the choices for `getLegalActions` (distinct objects, capped at `MAX_TARGET_COMBOS` = 48; "up to" slots offer 1..N targets). `play.ts` and `legalActions.ts` only call these now.
- `combat.ts`: `sideTotal` counts a stunned unit as 0 Might, so it deals no combat damage but can still be assigned damage.
- Events: `EFFECT_STUNNED`, `EFFECT_COUNTERED`, `GEAR_KILLED` (all public, no hidden info); a debuff is an `EFFECT_BUFF` with a negative amount; channeling reuses `RUNE_CHANNELED`. Client text in `client/src/game/describeEvent.ts` (+ test).
- Triggers: `parseTriggerText` now accepts every target-free clause (so "When you play me, deal N to all units at battlefields", "When I hold, you score 1 point", "channel 1 rune exhausted" in play/hold/Deathknell triggers).

## Known gaps / skipped

- **Tokens** ("Play a N [M] Bird/Sprite/Recruit unit token", "Play a Gold gear token exhausted", Bomb/Mech/Sand Soldier): the card DB has no unit or gear token definitions (only the Buff and XP Tracker TOKEN cards), so tokens need token CardDefs generated by the converter and an engine "create object" op (the kill path already removes tokens, 186.1). Skipped as a new subsystem. About 15 spells and 10 unit triggers wait on it.
- "Deal N to all enemy units at a battlefield" (Sprite-like "at a battlefield" without a battlefield choice) is not parsed: it probably means one chosen battlefield, which needs battlefield targets.
- Spells that need a choice of battlefield, "Move an enemy unit" (needs a destination), "Choose one -" modal spells, conditionals ("If this kills it", "If you do"), optional additional costs, "Look at the top N cards" (needs a hidden-information choice), Flow/Predict/Weaponmaster keyword spells, Take control, "Return a unit to hand".
- Triggers with targets or choices ("When you play me, choose an opponent / give a unit +N", "stun a unit") still need target selection for abilities.
- Counter ignores the cost conditions ("that costs no more than [4]") and Deflect-style targeting rules.
- `client/src/components/combat/damage.ts` (`sideTotalFromView`) does not yet treat a stunned unit as 0 Might; only the displayed side total differs, the engine decides who must assign damage.
- Not checked in a browser; the play popover lists one entry per target combination (up to 48).
